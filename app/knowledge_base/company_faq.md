## Return Policy
Customers can return any product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Refunds are sent to the
original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the item.

## Shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders over $50 and takes 3 to 5 business days.
Express shipping costs $15 and takes 1 to 2 business days.
We currently ship only within the United States and Canada.

## Warranty
All TechNova electronics come with a 1-year limited warranty that covers
manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage or water damage.
An extended 3-year warranty can be purchased for $49.

## Customer Support
Support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time.
Support is offered by email (support@technova.example) and live chat on our website only.
Phone support is not available.

## Account and Passwords
To reset your password, click "Forgot password" on the login page and follow
the link sent to your email. The reset link expires after 24 hours.
